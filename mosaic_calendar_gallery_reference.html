Design specification

The gallery itself is the home screen. No app title or logo inside the screen. Images should feel like collected fragments, not conventional media-management cards.

01

Month navigation

Current month is the only heading. Left/right controls change the calendar. The selected media filter remains active while navigating.

02

Horizontal filters

All, Movies, Books, Comics, Music, Podcasts, Games and Other. Use a compact pill row; scroll horizontally instead of wrapping into multiple lines.

03

Seven-column calendar

Monday-first calendar. Every date occupies a stable cell. Show a small, low-contrast date label and image thumbnails below it. Empty dates stay quiet.

04

Multiple records per day

Use a 1-up, 2-up or 2×2 miniature collage according to the number of entries. If there are more than four, show a subtle “+N” marker.

05

Add flow

Photo first, then type, title, optional creator, record date and optional personal note. Saving inserts the record into its actual date and category.

06

Visual tokens

Warm off-white background, near-black text, muted gray labels, hairline separators, restrained rounded controls and generous whitespace. No gradients on UI surfaces or heavy shadows.
